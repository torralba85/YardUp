## Design System

Always read DESIGN.md before making any visual or UI decisions.
The product center is ADU earning potential and builder connection, not design exploration alone.
All font choices, colors, spacing, hierarchy, and copy posture are defined there.
Do not restrict the app's framing to California unless the specific feature or data source is California-only.
In QA mode, flag any UI that hides revenue, cost, payback, or builder-readiness behind decorative design controls.
