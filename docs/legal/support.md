# YardUp Support

Contact: support@aduadvisor.app

YardUp (operated under the legal name ADU Advisor) is a planning aid for homeowners exploring accessory dwelling units. Users can:

- Request help with installation, sign-in, or in-app workflows
- Report bugs and submit feedback (the in-app feedback control sends the message directly to the YardUp team)
- Ask for deletion of cloud-backed records associated with their anonymous Firebase account
- Delete local-only project data by removing the app from the device

## What YardUp is not

YardUp does not provide:

- Stamped construction plans or signed architectural drawings
- Legal advice on zoning, easements, or HOA matters
- Financial, tax, or investment advice
- Guaranteed permit approval, builder availability, rental income, or resale value

All numbers shown in the app — rent estimates, cost ranges, payback, builder match counts, permit risk — are planning indicators. Verify with the local jurisdiction and licensed professionals before committing to construction work.

## Response time target

The team aims to respond to support requests within three business days. Critical issues affecting account access or data loss receive priority routing.
