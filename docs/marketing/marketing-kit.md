# YardUp Marketing Kit

Updated: 2026-05-24

This document tracks the marketing-surface briefs that follow the Quiet Ascent identity defined in [DESIGN.md](../../DESIGN.md). The goal is a unified visual language across paid, social, email, and organic touchpoints that balances financial professionalism with residential warmth.

## Brand Posture

- Lead with concrete earning potential and a credible builder path.
- Visual interest comes from property imagery, plan overlays, builder status, and revenue figures — not decorative gradients, orbs, or single-hue dashboards.
- Use the YardUp wordmark with `Up` in revenue green. Keep letter spacing `0`.
- Use "supported markets" language, not California-specific framing.

## Asset Briefs

### Social Media Card (1:1)

A professional 1080×1080 card for Instagram and LinkedIn. Feature a modern, minimalist ADU in a high-end residential backyard. Overlay copy:

- Headline: "Your Backyard, Unlocked."
- Subhead: "Estimate your revenue today."

Palette: Forest Deep and Revenue Green accents on a Paper background. Avoid architectural portfolio styling — frame the ADU as a financial asset.

### Display Banner (16:9)

A horizontal web ad showing a mobile device with the Value-tab dashboard (rent, cost range, payback, builders, packet readiness). Overlay copy:

- Headline: "Earn more from your property."
- CTA button: "Get Started"

Palette: Forest, Revenue Green, Paper. Aesthetic should read closer to a financial planning tool than a real-estate marketplace.

### Email Header (16:9)

A premium newsletter header with an architectural photo of a small modern studio ADU nestled in a green yard. The YardUp wordmark sits subtle in the upper-right corner. The image should feel trustworthy and professional — closer to a financial planning service than a design studio.

### Copy Pillars

When writing headlines, lead with one of the six revenue questions DESIGN.md defines:

1. How much could I make?
2. What would it cost?
3. How fast could it pay back?
4. What model fits?
5. What blockers exist?
6. Which builders can act on this?

Never imply guaranteed rental income, guaranteed permit approval, or guaranteed builder availability. Use ranges and "estimated" language.

## Production Notes

- Source images should be high-fidelity and avoid synthetic-looking AI artifacts. Prefer real ADU photography or restrained 3D renders.
- Generated 3D mockups must respect setbacks, lot coverage, and the buildable zone story — do not show ADUs on cars, sidewalks, or implausible placements.
- Keep CTA copy short. "Get Started", "See your range", and "Find a builder" all work; avoid "Unlock possibilities" and "Dream ADU".
- Marketing surfaces should link back to YardUp landing pages, not directly to the Next.js Gemini prototype.
