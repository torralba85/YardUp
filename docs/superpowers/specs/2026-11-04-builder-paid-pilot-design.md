## Proposed offer

"LotFoundry prepares three property-specific ADU concept presentations for your homeowner conversations: a shareable Studio layout, two clear images and a one-page summary for each property. Introductory pilot: $149 total."

Price is experimental, one-time and non-recurring. No certificate price changes or subscription. The service uses LotFoundry's existing configurable ADU; it does not reproduce the builder's proprietary floor plan or an exact prefab product.

For each of three eligible properties:

- One conceptual layout using the existing Studio dimensions, orientation, roof and finish controls.
- One working saved-layout URL, tested signed out. The recipient can interact with the existing free Studio; paid certificate features remain subject to the normal separate purchase.
- Two PNG views captured from the actual Studio: overview and ADU placement/detail, with visible concept/estimate labels and no invented photorealism.
- One-page PDF summary with the address, date, selected concept dimensions, visible preliminary cost/rent ranges and their basis/exclusions, unresolved issues, and the saved-layout link. Only use figures actually visible in the free Studio. Omit unavailable figures and explain why; never bypass a paywall or imply certification.
- One consolidated revision per property, limited to placement, supported dimensions/roof/finish, or factual corrections. It cannot make an unsuitable property pass or turn a concept into construction documents.

Disclosure on every summary and image: "Illustrative ADU concept, not an existing structure. Early planning estimates, not a permit, survey, engineering assessment or builder quote." Keep visible warnings in the Studio captures. No claim of increased sales, permit eligibility, verified setbacks or guaranteed income.

## Intake, acceptance and delivery

1. Builder provides three property addresses, desired approximate ADU dimensions/use, and confirms permission to supply and share the property information with intended recipients. Do not request homeowner phone/email, financing details or other unnecessary information.
2. Andrew checks each property in the existing Studio before accepting money. Initial scope is City of San Diego, one detached ADU concept per property. The real parcel must resolve, the location/jurisdiction must agree, and the existing structures and parcel data must be adequate to communicate a useful concept. A demo/preset substitution, unresolved address mismatch, missing critical geometry, or a collision requiring invention disqualifies the property. No manual concealment of warnings. Passing this delivery check does not establish legal feasibility.
3. Builder approves the specific three eligible addresses and written scope. If an address fails, offer a replacement before invoicing. Do not take payment until all three are accepted and the service sample/readiness checks pass.
4. Proposed payment: one manually prepared Stripe invoice for the $149 service, through LotFoundry's existing business account. Use a separate service description; do not route this through the $29 certificate checkout or grant certificate entitlements. Prepare only a draft until the buyer and Andrew confirm the order. Verify the account and final total before sending; no automatic charge or saved-card use. If invoicing is unavailable, stop payment setup and present the specific alternative for review rather than building a checkout.
5. Proposed turnaround: first versions of all three within three business days after payment and complete, accepted intake. Accept at most one paid builder in this first pilot, so delivery stays manageable. Start the delivery clock only after the readiness gate and Andrew's schedule confirmation.
6. Builder has seven calendar days from first delivery to submit one consolidated revision per property. Complete revisions within two business days of receiving clear requests. No extra fees or larger scope without a separate written quote accepted by the buyer.
7. Proposed refund handling: full refund on cancellation before preparation starts; after work starts, refund each undeliverable or cancelled, undelivered property at $49.67/$49.67/$49.66, totaling $149. Delivered work is not automatically refundable for a change of preference. Honor correction requests within scope. Andrew handles disputes and confirms every actual refund action. These are proposed commercial terms to approve, not a statement of statutory rights.

The service is manually delivered by Andrew with AI-assisted preparation and Andrew's final review. No external professional accreditation is represented. If a scope or accuracy issue prevents useful delivery, flag it and use replacement/refund handling; do not expand into professional planning advice.

## Experiment and evidence

Seven-day sales window starts when the sample is verified and the first approved outreach is sent, not at document creation. Preparation target is one working day, conditional on permissions, usable property data and browser/payment access. Failure of those dependencies is recorded, not treated as completed preparation.

Use a private local tracker, separate from the existing CRM unless Andrew requests otherwise. Record prospect, verified source/date, contact-history check, message review/send status, reply, sample requested/viewed if explicitly confirmed, offer, invoice, payment verified, accepted properties, delivery/revision dates, refund, net receipts and operator minutes. No unnecessary homeowner contact data. Do not infer sample views from email opens or bypass analytics consent.

Primary commercial signal: a genuine unrelated customer pays the service invoice. Record fulfillment separately: three delivered and reviewed packages, revision status and customer feedback. An unpaid invoice, free trial, owner test or enthusiastic response is not revenue. Track processing fees, refunds, direct costs and labor separately; $149 is gross revenue, not profit.

Proposed labor target: at most three hours for three properties, including review and normal revisions. Log setup/outreach time separately so acquisition effort is visible. If a sample suggests this is unrealistic, adjust scope/price before selling. With $149 gross and three delivery hours, the gross labor yield is about $49.67/hour before fees, acquisition time, tax and overhead; this is arithmetic, not an earnings forecast.

At seven days: if a customer paid, complete delivery before expanding, then ask what was useful and whether another package is worth buying. If no one paid, summarize replies, objections and actual conversations. Lack of replies does not prove lack of demand. Do not automatically add features, reduce price, buy traffic or build a marketplace. Recommend the next bounded test for Andrew to choose.
