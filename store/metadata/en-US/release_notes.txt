Welcome to YardUp.

This first release helps you answer the questions that matter before you call a builder: how much an ADU could earn, what it could cost, how fast it could pay back, and which builders can act on it.

What is in this release:
- Value tab with monthly rent estimate, net annual income, cost range, payback, builder matches, and quote packet readiness
- Estimate tab with gross rent, expenses, cost range, value range, payback, and sensitivity assumptions
- Builders tab with matched builders, license and insurance signals, and quote request flow
- Permits tab with risk level, blockers, verification steps, and source citations for supported markets
- Studio tab for backyard placement, ADU model fit, style direction, and concept generation with a deterministic fallback
- Guided onboarding wizard for address, lot facts, and goals
- Floating feedback control for sending short product feedback (rate-limited)
- Local-first project storage; cloud-backed actions only run when you request them

YardUp is a planning aid and does not guarantee rental income, permit approval, financial returns, or builder availability. Always verify with your local jurisdiction and licensed professionals.

YardUp is operated by ADU Advisor.
